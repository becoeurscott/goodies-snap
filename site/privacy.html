Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

This explains what goodiesSnap collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get rid of it. It matches what the app actually does.

The short version. We collect your email, your display name and what you post. Dish photos and imported text are sent to an AI provider to produce a recipe. We do not track you, we run no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data. You can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create your account, sign you in, and let you recover access.
Display nameShown next to your posts and comments.
Account identifierLinks your posts, likes, comments and AI allowance to you.
Photos you chooseDish photos are analysed to identify the food; photos you attach to a post are stored so the post can display them.
Content you createRecipes you share, captions, comments and group memberships.
AI usage recordsThe count, type and cost of AI actions, so your monthly allowance can be enforced.
Reports and blocksTo keep the community safe and to act on reports.
Subscription statusA purchase identifier from Apple, so your plan applies to your account.

Stays on your device: your saved recipe library, shopping list, meal plan and taste preferences. We do not upload them. They are not deleted when you delete your account — remove the app to remove them.

What we do not do

  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • No advertising, attribution, or third-party analytics SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • No location, contacts, health data, or background collection.

Who processes your data

  • InsForge — hosts our database, storage and server functions. Your account and community content live here.
  • OpenRouter, and the AI model providers it routes to (including Anthropic) — receive the photo or text you are importing so a recipe can be produced. Send only content you are comfortable sharing.
  • Apple — processes subscription payments. We never see your payment details; we receive only a purchase confirmation.

These are service providers acting on our instructions. We do not give anyone your data for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed immediately, along with your posts, comments, likes, group memberships and uploaded photos. Content reports may be retained after deletion where we need them to keep other users safe or to comply with the law.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete my account. It is permanent and takes effect immediately. If you cannot access the app, email us and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we will act within the time the law requires. The in-app deletion above satisfies a deletion request immediately.

Children

goodiesSnap is not for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is restricted at the database level so one account cannot read another's private records, and AI provider keys are held on our server and never shipped inside the app. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes

If we make a material change we will update the date above and notify you in the app before it takes effect.
